## Intro

I'm Cadence James, a network engineer based in the Pacific Northwest with a focus on automation and streamlining network operations. I specialize in Python, Powershell and automation in general. If there's a way to do something manually, there's almost certainly a way to automate it.

## How I See Things

- I believe in openness. Information wants to be free.
- I also believe in real privacy and security, not just the idea of it.
- Tech should just work. I like tinkering, but that should be a choice, not a requirement. It should also stay open and repairable. I back right to repair with my wallet. I own a [Framework](https://frame.work/) Laptop 13 and have a [Slate EV Truck](https://slate.auto) on pre-order.

## What I'm Doing

My homelab is the clearest example. It's unnecessary, but it works, and it's taught me a lot. I'm always improving it.

There's no grand plan behind my projects. They're just whatever I can DIY, whatever I enjoy, and whatever I'm in the mood for. My interests shift often, so I bounce between projects instead of sticking to one until it's finished.

What's next is open. After achieving my CCNP and earning my B.S., I'm in a bit of limbo. A Master's in Computer Science is on the table.
